# Recorded instrument samples

Every recording here is released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication) by its
authors, so it may be used, changed and redistributed freely, commercially or not,
without permission or attribution. We credit the authors anyway, with thanks.

The files are short excerpts (one take per note, one velocity layer, two for the piano, trimmed, mono,
96 kbps MP3), built by `scripts/build-sample-packs.ts`, which records the exact source
files and commits used.
